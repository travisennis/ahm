# Remove the procedure channel and the last prescription

## Summary

Delete `context.go` and `onboard.go` with the `context` and `onboard`
commands, prime's managed-work routing, `Managed Work Intake`, grooming and
audit hints, and the backlog-routing prose, plus status's onboarding snippet.
Before deleting them, salvage what the command prints: `ahm context task`
becomes `docs/workflow/tasks.md`, `ahm context plan` becomes
`docs/workflow/exec-plans.md`, and `ahm context adr` becomes
`docs/workflow/adrs.md`. These are hand-maintained project documents after the
change; the research procedure is dropped with its record family.
