# Rewrite documentation and instructions to the records boundary

## Summary

Bring every prose surface in line with ADR 022: `docs/VISION.md`,
`README.md`, `docs/README.md`, `docs/cli.md`, `docs/references/cli/*`,
`docs/references/workflow-spec.md`, `docs/references/glossary.md`,
`docs/guardrails/*`, `docs/guides/*`, `AGENTS.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, and
`ARCHITECTURE.md`. VISION's four channels and four record families are
replaced by the records-CLI boundary; the routing rules in `AGENTS.md` and
`CONTRIBUTING.md` that pointed at removed commands are replaced with the
procedure the project now owns, and the new `docs/workflow/` and
`docs/exec-plans/` surfaces are registered in the documentation map.
