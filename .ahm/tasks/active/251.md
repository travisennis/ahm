# Unify groom and audit delegated-result parsing and remove its quadratic scan

## Problem

Two coupled issues in the delegated-result parsers.

**Duplication.** `parseGroomResult` / `findGroomJSON` / `decodeGroomJSON`
(`task_groom.go:411-497`) and `parseAuditResult` / `findAuditJSON` /
`decodeAuditJSON` (`audit.go:167-236`) are near-verbatim copies. They differ
only in the target type and the required-field list — roughly 130 duplicated
lines that must be changed in lockstep whenever the delegated-response contract
moves.

**Quadratic scanning.** `findGroomJSON` and `findAuditJSON` call
`json.Marshal(value)` at *every node* while walking the decoded structure, and
`parseGroomResult` runs that whole walk over *every line* of the agent
transcript. Cost is O(lines x size^2), which on a large `stream-json` capture is
the difference between instant and unusable.

The duplication is why the performance fix would otherwise have to land twice.

## Relevant Files

- `internal/ahm/task_groom.go:411-497` — groom result parsing.
- `internal/ahm/audit.go:167-236` — audit result parsing.
- `internal/ahm/task_groom_test.go`, `internal/ahm/audit_test.go` — parser
  coverage.
- `internal/ahm/testdata/` — golden agent transcripts.
- `docs/guardrails/external-agent-orchestration.md` — parsing contract.

## Fix Direction

Collapse both into one generic `parseDelegatedResult[T]` parameterized by target
type and required-field list, then fix the scan once: decode each transcript
line directly into the target type, or marshal once at the top and walk the raw
bytes, instead of re-marshalling at every node.

Preserve the existing error messages and recovery behavior exactly —
task 207 (recovering invalid batch grooming verdicts) and task 211 depend on
specific failure modes, and the golden transcripts pin the parse contract.
